# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be welcoming and useful to everyone, whatever their background or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming a mistake was careless.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or unwelcome attention.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, open a private security advisory on the repository or contact the maintainer, [@darklight9811](https://github.com/darklight9811). Reports are handled in confidence.

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests and discussions.
